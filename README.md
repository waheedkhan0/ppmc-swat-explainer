# 🌊 Public Project Management Cycle (PPMC) Explainer Animation
### Comprehensive Walkthrough of the *Manual for Development Projects Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (2022)*
**Case Study:** *Upper Swat Rural Gravity-Solar Clean Drinking Water & Watershed Conservation Scheme*

An animated instructional presentation, interactive web application, and video synthesis engine explaining the complete 9-chapter Public Project Management Cycle (PPMC) in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa through a concrete, real-world development project.

---

## 📌 Project Profile
- **Project Title:** Upper Swat Rural Gravity-Solar Clean Drinking Water & Watershed Conservation Scheme
- **Location:** District Swat (Tehsils Matta & Bahrain), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
- **Sponsoring Agency:** Public Health Engineering Department (PHED), Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
- **Executing Agency:** Executive Engineer, PHED Division Swat
- **Target Output:** 42 decentralized gravity-piped and solar-lift water filtration stations supplying 150,000 rural residents
- **Estimated Capital Cost:** Rs. 1,200 Million (Rs. 1.2 Billion)
- **Implementation Period:** 36 Months (3 Years)

---

## 🏛️ Chapter-by-Chapter P&D Department Walkthrough

### Chapter 1: Socio-Economic Planning & Constitutional Foundations
- **Constitutional Mandate:** Grounded under Articles 37 & 38 (Principles of Policy, Constitution of Pakistan) to promote social justice and eradicate regional disparity through universal clean drinking water access.
- **KP PFM Act 2019 Section 14 Compliance:**
  - *Section 14(1):* Mandatory adherence to the formal project cycle without arbitrary shortcuts.
  - *Section 14(2):* Capital cost (Rs. 1.2 Billion) breaches statutory provincial thresholds, mandating a rigorous socio-economic Cost-Benefit Analysis prior to approval.

### Chapter 2: Public Sector Planning Machinery & Sanctioning Route
- **Grassroots Identification:** Bottom-up identification by Tehsil Development Committees (TDCs Matta & Bahrain), endorsed by the District Development Committee (DDC, DC Swat).
- **Competent Sanctioning Authority:**
  - DDC Limit: Rs. 50 Million (Breached)
  - DDWP Limit: Rs. 200 Million (Breached)
  - **Provincial Development Working Party (PDWP):** Holds statutory sanctioning jurisdiction for Rs. 1,200 Million funded via provincial ADP.
  - *Federal Coordination:* Escalates to CDWP/ECNEC only if PSDP cost-sharing or foreign assistance is requested.
- **Digital Registration:** Assigned unique digital tracking identity in the **Intelligent Project Automation System (i-PAS)** (`KP-2022-PHED-SWAT-042`).

### Chapter 3: Project Identification & Concept Clearance
- **Empirical Diagnostics:** PHED spring water pathogen testing (E. coli contamination) combined with the Swat District SDG Scorecard (child stunting rate at 41%).
- **Provincial Concept Clearance Committee (PCCC):** Submission of **Annexure 3-C (Project Concept Form for New Initiatives)** evaluating:
  1. Alignment with the Provincial PAMframe.
  2. Zero duplication with provincial irrigation or regional schemes.
  3. Long-term Operation & Maintenance (O&M) funding sustainability.
- *Outcome:* Formal PCCC Concept Clearance greenlights detailed engineering feasibility.

### Chapter 4: Project Preparation, Feasibility & Costing Standards
- **PC-II Statutory Study:** Capital cost exceeds Rs. 500 Million threshold $\to$ pre-qualified engineering consulting firm hired under KPPRA rules for hydrogeological yield testing, GIS pipeline topography, and Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA).
- **PC-I Rigid Costing Formula:**
  $$\textbf{Total Capital Cost = Base Cost + Physical Contingency (3\%) + Financial Price Contingency}$$
  - *Base Civil Works & Equipment:* Rs. 1,070.0 Million (Market Rate System / MRS).
  - *Physical Contingency (3%):* Rs. 32.1 Million (Strictly restricted to underground rock excavation & pipe alignment diversions; adding buildings is strictly prohibited).
  - *Financial Price Contingency:* Rs. 97.9 Million (compounded 6–8% annual inflation for cement, PVC, steel, and solar panels).
  - *Total Approved Capital Cost:* **Rs. 1,200.0 Million**.
- **3-Year Phasing:** Year 1: Rs. 350M | Year 2: Rs. 550M | Year 3: Rs. 300M.
- **Statutory Certification:** Signed by Executive Engineer and Administrative Secretary certifying field verification.

### Chapter 5: Project Appraisal & Economic Conversion
- **Six Appraisal Dimensions:** Technical, Institutional, Environmental, Financial, Social, and Economic.
- **Shadow Price Conversion Parameters:**
  - *Standard Conversion Factor (SCF = 0.90):* Non-traded domestic materials (Rs. 600M market cost) converted to **Rs. 540 Million** economic cost (removing 10% indirect taxes and markups).
  - *Shadow Wage Rate Factor (SWRF = 0.80):* Unskilled labor (Rs. 100M market wage) converted to **Rs. 80 Million** economic cost (reflecting rural underemployment).
- **Social Valuation:** Monetized health savings from reduced typhoid/diarrhea admissions plus 2.5 hours/day time saved by women fetching water.
- **Appraisal Decision Rule (Social Discount Rate = 10%):**
  - Financial IRR (FIRR) = 4.2% (Heavily subsidized water tariffs).
  - **Economic IRR (EIRR) = 15.8%** (Clears 10% hurdle rate!).
  - **Net Present Value (NPV) = +Rs. 240 Million (> 0)** $\to$ Formally Sanctioned by PDWP.

### Chapter 6: Project Implementation, Governance & Revisions
- **Administrative Approval (AA):** Issued by PHED Administrative Department.
- **Fund Release:** 100% of Year-1 allocation (Rs. 350 Million) credited into a dedicated **ASAAN Assignment Account** at the National Bank of Pakistan (NBP).
- **Governance:** Project Director hired competitively under KP Project Policy (Annexure 6-AA) on Project Pay Scale (PPS-9); tenders run via KPPRA Rules 2014.
- **The Non-Resetting 15% Revision Rule (Annexure 6-CD):**
  - *Year 2:* Flash flood damages valve station $\to$ +Rs. 96 Million (+8%) cost increase approved locally by DDWP ($\le 15\%$).
  - *Year 3:* Steel & solar market price surge $\to$ +Rs. 96 Million (+8%) cost increase requested.
  - *Rule Applied:* Cumulative escalation is calculated against original approved baseline ($8\% + 8\% = 16\%$). Because 16% breaches the 15% statutory ceiling, DDWP jurisdiction is void. PHED must submit a **Revised PC-I** to the PDWP for fresh sanction.

### Chapter 7: Results-Based Monitoring (RBM) & DG M&E
- **CREAM Benchmark Indicator:** *"Percentage of target households receiving 45 liters/capita/day of potable water meeting WHO standards"* (Clear, Relevant, Economic, Adequate, Monitorable).
- **Periodic Tracking:** Quarterly Form PC-III submissions in i-PAS tracking physical milestone completion (65% pipes laid) vs financial disbursements (60%).
- **Directorate General of M&E (DG M&E):** Independent unannounced field inspections with GPS timestamping, aerial drone verification, and laboratory concrete core testing to prevent fictitious billing.

### Chapter 8: Project Closure & Asset Handover
- **Reconciliation & Contingency Surrender:** Audited final cost: Rs. 1,180 Million. Remaining Rs. 20 Million physical contingency savings strictly surrendered to the **Provincial Consolidated Fund**. ASAAN account formally closed.
- **PC-IV Project Completion Report:** Submitted with as-built engineering drawings within statutory deadlines.
- **Transition to Recurrent Budget:** 42 stations transferred from development budget to PHED's regular non-development (revenue) budget with recurring Operation & Maintenance (O&M) allocations for chlorine, solar maintenance, and staff.
- **Post-Completion Evaluation (PC-V):** Filed annually for 3 years post-handover tracking clinic admissions.

### Chapter 9: Emerging Initiatives Mainstreamed
- **Gender Equality Marker (GEM):** Scores **Tier-3** on the GEM checklist; women directly participated in tap-stand placements, reducing fetch times and boosting girls' school attendance.
- **Nutrition Marker:** Tagged as **Nutrition-Sensitive**; eliminating waterborne diarrhea prevents chronic gut malabsorption and child stunting.
- **Global SDG Mapping:** Direct impact on **SDG 6** (Clean Water & Sanitation), **SDG 3** (Good Health & Well-being), and **SDG 5** (Gender Equality).
- **District SDG Scorecard:** Direct empirical data upgrade for District Swat's official scorecard.

## 💻 Tech Stack & Structure

- **Frontend & Visuals:** Pure HTML5, Responsive CSS3, Procedural SVG Animation, Stick-Figure Pose Rigging.
- **Audio Synthesis:** Edge-TTS Neural Voice Engine (`en-US-GuyNeural`), Web Audio SFX synthesis (gavels, alerts, chimes, water).
- **Video Rendering:** Python Playwright (headless browser driving frame-accurate animations) + FFmpeg H.264/AAC muxer.

```
animations/
├── .gitignore
├── README.md
└── ppmc_swat_explainer/
    ├── index.html            # Main interactive presentation player
    ├── styles.css            # Animation keyframes & theme styles
    ├── app.js                # State machine, procedural SVG director & Web Audio
    ├── generate_audio.py     # Edge-TTS speech generation script
    ├── update_metadata.py    # Duration & timestamp synchronization
    ├── test_visuals.py       # Playwright visual QC test
    ├── render_video.py       # Chrome + FFmpeg MP4 export engine
    ├── audio/                # 10 chapter voiceover MP3 files & JSON metadata
    ├── qc/                   # Visual QC screenshots of each chapter
    └── out/                  # Rendered MP4 video outputs
```
